# SuperstorePowerBIAnalysis

## Overview: 
The project shows an interactive Power BI Exploratory Data Analysis report of a superstore. This project helps achieve an understanding of the store's revenue, products, and global popularity, with insights that can be useful for the organisation's growth and development. 

## Objectives: 
- Performing Exploratory Data Analysis(EDA) on the dataset of the superstore to develop data and business understanding. 
- Identifying key trends in sales, products and regional distribution. 
- Generating functional insights. 

## Data Source: 
- The dataset(the .xlsx file) was imported from Kaggle. 

## Workflow and components: 
- The Excel file downloaded from Kaggle 
- Necessary visualizations were added to the report, such as 
 * category and product popularity 
 * regional engagement 
 * yearly profits 
along with respective insights.  
- Slicers of the important domains to view and analyse data of individual entries. 

## Key insights: 
-Sales show a positive trend throughout the years. 
-Phones, copiers, chairs and bookcases are the top products of the superstore and make up almost half(48.92%) of the sales. 
-Asia Pacific is the largest contributing market, while Western Europe is the largest contributing Region. 
-The superstore and shown consistent profits throughout the years at 11%.
